Topic: Projects

You want your open assigned issues, not every issue mentioning a topic.

<summary>Explanation</summary>

In GitHub issue search, use is:issue is:open assignee:@me. Add a repository filter when needed. This is a search query, not a terminal command. No results may simply mean nothing matches.
