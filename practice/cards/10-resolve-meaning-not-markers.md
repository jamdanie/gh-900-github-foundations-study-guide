# Resolve meaning, not markers

Topic: Git

A conflict offers two different descriptions. Is removing the conflict markers enough?

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>

No. Choose final content that makes sense and verify it. Stage the resolved file and finish the merge. Git can record your resolution but cannot decide whether the explanation is accurate.

</details>
