# A draft with passing checks

Topic: Collaboration

Your draft PR has green checks. Can it merge while it remains a draft?

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>

No. Mark it ready when it is ready for review, then satisfy applicable merge requirements. Marking ready does not itself merge the PR or replace review.
