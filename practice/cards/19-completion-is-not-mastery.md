# Completion is not mastery

Topic: Projects

Every task in a study milestone is closed. Does its completion percentage prove exam readiness?

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>

No. It measures the state of those work items. Someone could close a task after reading without being able to apply the concept. Use new scenarios and hands-on checks to examine understanding.

</details>
