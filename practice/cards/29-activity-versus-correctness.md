# Activity versus correctness

Topic: Security and evidence

A repository has many commits and stars. Does that prove its study explanations are correct?

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>

No. Activity and interest are different from accuracy. Read the sources, inspect examples, and try the exercises. A contribution graph records qualifying activity; it does not grade the usefulness of each change.

</details>
